Our mission

A place to
try things.

Innova’Lab brings together students at ISET Medenine who want to move from consuming technology to understanding, shaping and creating with it.

We believe students learn best with the freedom to explore, the support to keep going, and a community to build with. No grades, no pressure: just real projects and good people.
